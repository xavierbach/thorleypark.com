Werribee Open Range Zoo
Wednesday · 8 July
8:15 AM Werribee Open Range Zoo
K Road, Werribee South · ~1 hr 15 min drive

Aim to leave by around 8:15 to get there for opening. The Safari Bus tour takes you through the open range with rhinos, zebras, giraffes and ostriches. Plan around 30–40 min for the safari, then walking circuits afterwards (lions, gorillas, hippos).

It's the longest drive of the week, but worth it. Food on-site is fine but the cafe gets busy at midday. Pack snacks to keep things flexible.

Let me know if you'd like to do this one and I'll buy tickets in advance.

Trentham Falls
Wednesday · 15 July
10:00 AM Trentham Falls picnic
Falls Road, Trentham · ~25 min drive

Beautiful 32-metre waterfall in the Wombat State Forest, easy to reach via a short walk from the carpark. A nice local outing — pack a thermos and lunch, there are picnic tables at the lookout.

Pop into Trentham itself afterwards if you want a wander — the bakery and a few cafes are worth a stop. Could also extend the drive to Daylesford or Hepburn Springs if Ruby's keen for more.

Finn at kinder by default — but if you'd like him along for the picnic, easy to keep him home for the day.
