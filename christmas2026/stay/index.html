Where to stay

Sleep in the
Macedon Ranges

Mount Macedon is about an hour north-west of Melbourne. For interstate guests, we strongly recommend staying the night before and possibly the night after. Below are our picks across a range of budgets — all within 15 minutes of Thorley Park. Book well in advance: Christmas is popular in the Ranges.

Luxe
Mollisons Kyneton
Kyneton — 18 min drive
The finest boutique accommodation in the region. Six hotel-style ensuite rooms and a spacious two-bedroom apartment, all individually decorated with antique pieces. Walking distance to Kyneton’s Piper Street restaurant strip.
Ensuite rooms from ~$240/night Apartment from ~$320/night

Luxe
Hanging Rock Views
Near Woodend — 12 min drive
Four luxury suites with spectacular Hanging Rock views, forest walks from your door, and several award-winning wineries within minutes. Stunning setting and superb quality.

Mid-range
Northfield Woodend
Woodend — 10 min drive
Two immaculate, entirely private boutique apartments in a quiet corner of Woodend village. King beds, generous bathrooms, and a short walk to the town’s cafes and shops. Quiet and well-appointed.
1 bedroom per apartment Walking distance to Woodend

Mid-range
Islay House
Woodend — 10 min drive
An iconic historic property right in the heart of Woodend. Beautifully renovated with heritage character and modern comfort. Great for families or groups wanting to be close to the village.

Hesket Estate Cottage
Hesket — 15 min drive
A charming self-contained rural cottage on a working vineyard, overlooking paddocks and vines. Peaceful, private, and genuinely beautiful. Perfect for couples wanting a proper country escape.

Value
The Black Forest Motel
Woodend — 10 min drive
A fastidiously renovated boutique motel with genuine character — the “think motel, think again” option. Great value, clean, and well-located in Woodend. Excellent reviews for comfort and friendliness.
