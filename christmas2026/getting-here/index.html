Getting here

Finding
Thorley Park

Mount Macedon — about an hour north-west of Melbourne. Driving directions, V/Line train option, parking notes, and tips for those flying in.

Address

Thorley Park — 49 Alton Road, Mount Macedon VIC 3441

By Car from Melbourne

Take the M8 (Western Ring Road) or M1 (Calder Freeway) to Calder Park Drive, then north on the Calder Freeway to the Mount Macedon Road exit. From there, follow Mount Macedon Road up the mountain and turn onto Alton Road. Total drive time: approximately 55–70 minutes depending on traffic. We recommend giving yourself extra time on Christmas morning.

By Train

V/Line Bendigo line trains run regularly from Southern Cross Station to Woodend Station (approx. 1 hour). From Woodend, Mount Macedon is a 10-minute taxi or rideshare. Pre-book a local taxi or rideshare app — availability may be limited on Christmas Day.

Parking

We have ample off-street parking at the property. Drive in via the main gate on Alton Road. If you’re planning to have a few drinks (as you should), consider staying overnight locally and leaving the car — it’s a lovely morning drive up the mountain.

From the Airport

Melbourne Airport (Tullamarine) is approximately 40 minutes south via the Calder Freeway. Rideshare services operate from the airport directly to Mount Macedon. If you’re flying in for Christmas, consider arriving the day before and staying at one of the nearby properties listed on the Stay page.
