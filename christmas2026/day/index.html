The day

How the day
will unfold

A casual start, a long lunch, and an unhurried afternoon. Friday, 25 December 2026 — from late morning until whenever.

11:30 am
Arrive & Settle In
Gates open. Find your spot in the garden, help yourself to drinks, and let the kids loose. A casual start — no rush.
12:00 pm
Champagne & Canapés
Sparkling on arrival. Fresh king prawns with cocktail sauce and lemon, cold-smoked salmon blinis, and a selection of cheeses to graze from until lunch is served.
1:00 pm
Secret Santa Draw
We’ll gather briefly to reveal this year’s Secret Santa pairings. Gifts are kept until after lunch — the reveal is the fun part.
2:00 pm
Lunch is Served
The main event. Everyone takes their seat for a proper sit-down feast — roast turkey, glazed ham, all the trimmings. Long table. Long afternoon.
4:00 pm
Dessert & Gifts
Christmas pudding, pavlova, and the chaos of Secret Santa unwrapping. Cold drinks, comfortable chairs, and nowhere to be.
Evening
Stay as Long as You Like
The garden is yours. Cheese board replenished, wine flowing, kids eventually winding down. No formal finish time — Christmas ends when it ends.
