    How it works

    1

    Pick your water

    Switch between freshwater and saltwater. The palette and the tank water change to match — plants and tropical fish on one side, corals and reef fish on the other.

    2

    Build the scape

    Drop in hardscape, plants or corals, livestock, equipment and decor. Choose a substrate, slope it toward any corner, then orbit in full 3D and drag, resize, spin, tilt and raise each piece — everything stays inside the glass.

    3

    Check the build

    The build report tracks your stocking level against the tank size and runs a care checklist — heater, lighting, filtration, flow, skimmer and more.

    4

    Save & share

    Your layout auto-saves in your browser. Export a PNG to share the look, or save a file you can reload later.

    A planning aid for fun and inspiration — always research the specific care needs and compatibility of any livestock before you buy. Stocking estimates are rough guides, not gospel.
